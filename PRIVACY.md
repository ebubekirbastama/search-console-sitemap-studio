# Privacy Policy — Sitemap Studio

Sitemap Studio is a local browser extension. It does not have a backend service, send analytics, use cookies for tracking, or transmit user data to its author.

## Data stored

The extension stores the following locally in `chrome.storage.local`:
- Search Console property addresses entered by the user.
- Sitemap path.
- Submission job state, timestamps, property-level results and error logs.
- Selected interface language.

The extension communicates with Google Search Console pages at `search.google.com` to submit the sitemap on behalf of the signed-in user. Google processes that interaction under Google's own terms and policies. The extension does not read passwords or export browser cookies.

## Data sharing

No data is sold or shared by the extension author. If you choose to export a log file, the log is downloaded to your device and remains under your control. Logs may contain your property addresses and error details; review them before sharing publicly.

## Data deletion

Click **Reset progress** in the popup to remove the saved job state and logs. Your editable property list and settings remain. To remove all saved extension data, uninstall the extension or clear its storage through Chrome.

## Contact

For issues or privacy questions, use the GitHub repository's Issues section once it is published.
